About Me

My name is Keegan Li, a senior at Columbia University studying Computer Science. I'm looking to pursue a career in machine learning, AI, or software engineering upon my graduation.

My academic research spans two labs: at the Yang Lab, I work on detecting the origins of LLM hallucinations by developing diagnostic frameworks to analyze discrepancies between ground truth and simulated truth. At the CRIS Lab, I analyze the geometric processing space of LLMs and how they process emotions in text. I'm implementing sparse autoencoders to analyze n-gram probability distributions across GPT-2 Small and TinyBERT, providing insights into how different model architectures encode semantic information.

Professionally, I've interned as a machine learning engineer at Muse in Summer 2024, where I used fine-tuning and RAG to build an automated accounting agent. I fine-tuned Meta LLaMa 3 and Anthropic Claude models with AWS SageMaker and implemented Retrieval-Augmented Generation using AWS BedRock and OpenSearch to enhance chatbot responses. In 2023, I gained valuable industry experience through a software engineering internship at AI Media Technologies, where I developed automated API testing algorithms for RESTful API testing and created data pipelines with AWS Lambda.

Outside the classroom, I'm a hammer thrower on the Columbia University NCAA Track and Field team. I am also a saxophonist in the Columbia Wind Ensemble. Apart from track and music, I also enjoy playing poker, and am active in the Columbia Poker Club.
